It is the process of getting things done as __efficiently__ and __effectively__ as possible __with__ and __through people__ by guiding their efforts towards common objectives.
__Efficiency__ is getting work done with a minimum effort, expense or waste.
__Effectiveness__ is accomplishing tasks that help full organizational objectives.
Management uses resources to accomplish the organization's objectives.
## Types of Resources

1. Human resource - people
2. Capital or financial resources - money
3. Physical resources - raw materials
4. Informational resources - knowledge

## Features of Management

1. Management is __goal-oriented__- this means the success of any organization is measured by the achievement of their objectives.
2. Management is __continuous__- management is the continuous identification of business problems then developing and applying appropriate solutions in order to maximize production.
3. Management is __universal__- whether the organization is a social, political or economic it requires management.
4. Management is a __group activity__ - management involves the use of group effort in order to achieve objectives/goals.
__Universal nature of management__ - management principles are universal in nature. This means they are applicable in any institution i.e government, private, military, educational or any other organization involved in the production of goods and services.

## Importance of Management

1. Improves the lives of workers - this is through financial and non-financial incentives.
2. It encourages initiative- when employees take initiative it can give the organization success and satisfaction the employees.
3. It facilitates growth and expansion- this is through increasing efficiency.
4. Motivating the workers - through financial and non-financial incentives.
5. It reduces wastage- through proper planning and control resource wastage is minimized.
6. It increases efficiency- through resource wastage minimization and experimentation.
7. It improves relations- better relations leads to better teamwork and success.
