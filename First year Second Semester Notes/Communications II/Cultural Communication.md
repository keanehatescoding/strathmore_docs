__Communication__ how individuals express themselves.
__Intercultural communication__ is Communication that takes place between different cultural backgrounds but living in the same country.
__Cross cultural communication__ happens when parties engage in negotiation or any other form of communication but these parties are from different countries or backgrounds.
__Multicultural communication__ -occurs when people from different cultural, ethnic, racial and religious backgrounds communicate with one another by making special adjustments to different cultural standards through tolerance and sensitivity.
__Cross cultural pragmatics__ what is implied vs what is actually said. i.e bite me means a like metaphorically fight me in American culture but in other cultures it could mean fight me.
__Intercultural communication competence__ is the ability to communicate effectively and appropriately in various cultural context.
__Ethnocentrism__ the belief that your culture is better than other's.

__High context culture__ a culture where usually a lot of the meaning comes from non-verbal and contextual cues(facial expression, body language)
__Dominant culture__ attitudes,beliefs, values and customs that are held by the majority of people in a society
__Co cultures__ are groups of people living within a dominant culture but demonstrating communication that is sufficiently different to differentiate them from each other.
__Prejudice__ preconceived ideas/beliefs that are not based on actual facts and are usually negative.
__Stereotypes__ generalizations that do not allow for exceptions.
__Etiquette in non verbal behaviours__ understanding the meaning of gestures, facial expressions and body language requiring specific knowledge of a culture.
__Environment__ is how individuals view and relate to other people,objects and issues in their sphere of influence.
__Time__ how individuals perceive the nature of time and its use.
__Actions__ how individuals view actions and interactions.
__Competitiveness__ how individuals are motivated.
__Race__ is a “A local geographic or global human population distinguished as a more or less distinct group by genetically transmitted physical characteristic
__Ethnicity__ is the acceptance of the group mores and practices of one’s culture of origin and the concomitant sense of belonging.
__Nationality__ the status of belonging to a particular nation by origin, birth, or naturalization.
