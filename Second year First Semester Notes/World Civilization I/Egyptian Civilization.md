Emergence: 3500 BC
Reason: Nile river civilization
It is divided into 3:
1. The old kingdom
2. The middle kingdom
3. The new kingdom
In 3100 BC, the lower and upper, the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt by Pharaoh Menes which was separated by a Cataract(waterfall).
This kingdom comprised to 31 dynasties which spanned 2600 years.
__Pharaoh__ mean __Great House__.

## The Old Kingdom
(Dynasties I - XI)
Characterized by great pyramid building i.e the great pyramid of Giza.
As time progressed they pharaohs stopped building larger and larger pyramids.
The sphinx was also build during this time.

## The Middle Kingdom
(Dynasties XII-XIV)
Centered at Thebes
In the XI dynasty they saw a revival of Egypt's commercial Spirit.
in the XII dynasty the cities, temples were restored, crime repressed and taxation was readjusted.
The XIII and XIV dynasty struggle with Hyskos(Asiatic) invasions and the Hyskos period.
Ahmes I of the XVIII dynasty squashed the Hyskos leading to the new kingdom.

## The New Kingdom
(Dynasties XVIII-XXXI)
Dynasty XVIII is known as the Asiatic Empire due to the influx of Asiatics who intermarried with the Egyptions for Centuries.
Also during this time the Hebrews settled in Egypt that were initially treated well before being enslaved.
The exodus took place during the reign of Merenptah.
This period was followed by prolong foreign domination first by the Nubians.
Egypt was conquered by Assyria in 650 BC, however they were expelled and Egyptions restored their kingdom under Necto II.
Egypt then fell under persian dominion and later Macedonian rule by Alexander the Great unopposed.
When Alexander the Great died Egypt were dominated by the Ptolemies who ruled for the next 3 centuries.
The Ptolemies built the University of Alexandria.
    • Ptolemy II opened canal between Red Sea  the Nile
    • He also built roads from Red Sea ports to the Nile for commercial purposes. 
    • He allowed translation of Hebrew scriptures into Greek (Septuagint) 
    • He also oversaw the building of Alexandrian  Lighthouse on the island of Pharos 
    • The last Ptolemy was Cleopatra whose weaknesses made Egypt to be a province of Rome. 

### Achievements
1. __Economics__- creation of a copper currency system, __deben__ as the inhabitants moved from a food gathering system to a food processing system and took part in trade.
2. __Crafts, sculptures and paintings__ - were located in temples that depicted trade, family, farming and religious ceremonies. Also pharaohs were much larger then regular people in this and the Sphinx portrayed the pharaoh as a powerful lion.
3. __Mummification__ - pharaohs believed in an afterlife, they belived Osiris weighed a person's heart against a feather of truth in order to win eternal life. Also mummification was to give a soul use of its body in the after life.
4. __Surgery__ - from mummification they developed surgery technique. I.e removal of vital organs like the brain via the nostril learnt by mummification.
5. __Medicine__- they had doctors who could diagnose a patient's symptoms and provide treatment.
6. __Mathematics__ - they developed the decimal system. Could calculate area of polygons and volume of a cylinder and a hemisphere.
7. __Astronomy__ - Using movement of the heavens they formulated a calendar and they invented the 365 day calendar that is used in modern day.
8. __Architecture__ - They applied their mathematical knowledge to extract, transport and position huge blocks of stone in their architectural projects such as palaces, pyramids, tombs and temples.
9. __Writing__ - hieroglyphics system of writing and they moved from writing on stone to papyrus based paper.
10. __Philosophy__ - priests believed the universe is controlled by the human mind, concept of the eternal universe among others.
11. __Education__ - it was open to all classes, poor men/women could eclipse aristocrats as long as they showed greater merit. Also university of Alexandria built by Ptolemy II.
12. __Class system__ which at the top was pharaoh, high priests/priestess, nobles(soldiers), merchants, scribes, artisans, wealthy farmers who enjoyed many exemptions and privileges. At the bottom were peasant farmers and slaves, though slaves could become soldiers and gain freedom and upgrade their social class.
13. __Literature__ - older literature consists of hymns and songs, proverbs, love poems and while other writings tell of royal victories in battle.
14. __Government__ - centralized system of administration headed by a pharaoh, who appointed a Vizier(Chief Minister) that had governors and bureaus that presided over irrigation, tax collection, farming etc.
15. __Law and justice__ - minor cases were punished by fines and exile and handled by the Kenbet(Concil of Elders) while major cases were handled by the Pharaoh or Vizier and had serious consequences i.e decapitation or impairment.
