__Population__ a set of all elements of interest in a particular study.
__Sample__ a subset of the population.
__Parameter__ a numerical measure that describes a  characteristic of a __population__.
__Statistics__ a numerical measure that describes a characteristic of a __sample__.
__Variable__ is a characteristic or attribute that can assume different values.
__Data__ are the facts and figures collected, summarized, analyzed, and interpreted.
__Dataset__ a collection of data.
__Sampling__ is a process used in statistical analysis for selecting a sample from the population.
