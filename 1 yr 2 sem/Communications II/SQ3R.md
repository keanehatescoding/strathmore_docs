This is a reading technique that is widely known and is flexible.
It consists the following stages:
## 1. Survey
At this stage you speed read, scan and skim the text. The purpose of this is to gain the general gist of the material.

## 2. Question
Before reading any text you should have a set of questions that you will guide you. This questions can help you consider which information you want to retain. It also changes reading from a passive to an interactive session. Also read the questions at the end of the text.(If it has)

## 3. Read
The reading stage involves actively reading the material. By active we mean answering the questions from the previous stage and keeping in mind iitalicised, underlined or bold words you have made or were already present in the text.

## 4. Recite/Recall
Unless you constantly use or recite the information that you just read you will eventually forget what you just read. Recalling gives you the chance to think about the key points in the text.  Recalling can also be done through annotation.
__NB: Annotating__ this is writing down in your own words the key points in the text.

## 5. Review
At this stage you question your recall capabilities. To do this you can create flashcards, use mnemonics. Review makes you challenge your understanding and recall capabilities of the text.
