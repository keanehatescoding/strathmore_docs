In angle modulation we change the __frequency__ or __phase__ of the carrier signal with respect to the modulating signal.
Angle modulation can be classified into two parts:
1. Frequency modulation
2. Phase modulation

## Advantages
1. Noise reduction since it has a lower Signal to Noise Ratio compared to amplitude modulation.
2. More efficient user of power compared to amplitude modulation
3. Improved system fidelity

## Applications
1. Radio broadcasting.
2. TV Sound transmission.
3. Cellular radio transmission.
