Consider a vector $\vec {a}$ that is defined as $$
\vec{a}=
\begin{bmatrix} a_{1} \\ a_2 \\ .. \\ a_{n-1} \\a_{n}\end{bmatrix}
$$
and other vector $\vec{b}$ that is defined as
$$\vec{b}=\begin{bmatrix} b_{1}\\ b_{2} \\.. \\ b_{n-1}\\ b_{n}\end{bmatrix}$$
Then the dot product of the two vectors $\vec a \cdot \vec b$ is given by
$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}= a_{1}b_{1}+a_{2}b_{2}+..+a_{n-1}b_{n-1}+a_{n}b_{n}$$
where n is the degree of the vectors.
