In his work, the Rhetoric. Aristotle outlined these 3 elements that any person can use to convince another person about a metter.
## 1. Logos
Use of reason(logic) to persuade the listener/reader. Pathos can be done through stating facts, statistics, examples to persuade your audience.

## 2. Pathos
Involves appealing to the emotions of the audience. Pathos can be practised by:
1. Using vivid and evocative language.
2. Using imagery
## 3. Ethos
Refers to the credibility of the speaker. It can be manifested through certifications, endorsements by reputable people and demonstration of expertise.
