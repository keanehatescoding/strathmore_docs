This is the first function of management.
It is the process of selecting objectives, goals and missions then the strategies(actions) to achieve them.
or 
It is determining in advance what should be accomplished and how it should be realized.
A __plan__ is a __predetermined course of action__ for a achieving objectives.
The are four forms of planning:
1. Strategic planning
2. Tactical planning
3. Contingency planning
4. Operational planning

## Purpose of planning

1. To cater for the growing complexity of the business environment.
2. Plans act as base for decision making.
3. To ensure efficient use of resources.
4. Plans cater for the growth of the business.
5. It reduces uncertainty.
6. Minimizes wastage of resources or redundancy.
7. It sets the standards for controlling.
8. To cater for growing complexity of managerial jobs.

## Elements of Planning

1. Goals
2. Objectives
3. Strategies
4. Resources
5. Implementation
6. Evaluation
## Strategic planning

Concerned with the setting of broad, long-range goals by top managers.

## Steps

1. Define purpose/mission
2. Setting organization goals/objectives
3. Formulating strategic plan
4. Implementing the strategic plan
5. Evaluating the strategic plan
Reasons for failure
2 Main reasons are:
1. Having inappropriate strategies
Incomplete SWOT (Strength, Weakness, Opportunities, )
2. Poor implementation
Overestimation of resources
Underestimation of time
Importance of strategic planning
1. Gives the top management the ability to search long time goals
2. Helps anticipate problems even before they rise.
3. Minimizes the chances of making mistakes
4. Helps you to make better decisions.

## Tactical Planning
This identification of specific, short-range objectives by lower-level managers.

## Operational Planning

The setting of work standards and schedules.
## Contingency Planning

Identification of backup plans in case the main plan fails.

# Management by Objectives Approach

For MBO to be successful, 3 things have to happen:
1. The commitment of Top Management is essential
2. It must be applied Organization-wide
3. Objectives must cascade- at top level there must be the long term goals, at the middle level there must be tactical plans and at the low level there must be more tactical plans.

## Steps

1. Employees and managers agree on their objectives.
2. Mangers develop action plans.
3. Mangers and employees periodically review the employees performance.
4. Manager appraises the employees

## Merits

1. Clarifies to individual what is expected of them
2. Helps managers set achievable performance
3. Improves motivation
4. Improves team Spirit

## Demerits

1. It is expensive
2. It is also difficult when setting goals when the employees are not trained in setting goals
3. Kills creative and innovative spirit since individuals are only motivated in achieve the stated goals using the specified methods agreed.
4. The manager goal setting process requires a very high level of skills in interpersonal relationship
