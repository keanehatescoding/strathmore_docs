
## Cartesian Product of Two Relation

The Cartesian Product of relations R and S is the set that is a concatenation of the tuples from R and S.
The degree of the Cartesian Sum is defined as the sum of the degree of the participating relations.
The Cardinality is defined by the product of the participating relations.

## Relational Algebra Expression
Branches X Staff 
## Oracle PL/SQL Equivalent Expression

```SQL
SELECT * FROM Branch,STAFF
```
OR 
```SQL
SELECT * FROM STAFF
CROSS JOIN STAFF
```
