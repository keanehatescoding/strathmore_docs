## Olmec Civilization
It is the oldest of the ancient Meso-American civilization therefore not much is known about it.
They flourished between 1500 BC and 400AD
Place: Teotihuacan or modern day Mexico
Reason for destruction: Probably invasion from a group of invaders from the South.

### Social History
They practised chocolate drinking.
Various artifacts have been found that are been linked to this Civilisation namely downturned mouths, cleft heads, werejaguars, figurines etc made of jade, clay, basalt and greenstone among others
The most recognized of aspect of the Olmec Civilization are the enormous helmeted heads.
The ruling class wore Jade face masks.
They practised bloodletting.
They developed a form of writing.
### Economic History

They practised subsistence farming.
Once they began forming cities they emerged a elite class.
They had very sophisticated architecture that consists of great pyramids.
The ruling class wore Jade face masks.
### Decline
They declined probably because of serious environmental catastrophe that rendered the region unsuitable for subsistence farming. 
Another factor that is believed to have caused this is volcanism.

## Mayan Civilization
It is located in the Yucatan Peninsula therefore they had to practice slash and burn in order to get farm land.
They were mostly subsistence farmers for most of the period.
They had pictographical writing which was syllabic rather than alphabetic. They also had a Mayan calender.
They had sports in the form of a game known as Pok-A-Tok which involved passing a rubber ball into a high hoop without the use of hands and feet.
They borrowed a lot from the Olmec i.e their gods, rituals, art.
There architecture was quite advanced and constituted of pyramids.
They took part in heavily in trade.
They had a social class system.
Rulers had unquestioned power.
Mayan kings were fierce war captain.
The Nacom was a chief military strategist responsible for gathering and organizing armies.
They use spears, clubs, axes during war.
They heavily engaged in warfare in order to get resources, acquire prisoners for human sacrifices, control trade routes.
Human sacrifices were only reserved for special occasions i.e Cornation, Diseases, drought.
Their religion was polytheistic.
They were not complete subdued by the spanish but most assimilated to Catholicism and mixed part of their culture with catholicism.
## Aztec
They only lasted 100 years before they were wiped out by the Spanish.
There capital was Tenochtilan.
Kings controlled cities, and they regulary took part in wars in order to subdue other cities in order to pay tribute to them.
They had a fierce and strong army. Whenever they conquered any group they included their conquered tribes into their army.
Moctezuma I began the construction of the most important Aztec Temples.
They had a social class system that was divided into:
1. The sovereign (tlahtoani)
2. The dignitaries( close friends/relatives of the tlahtoani)
3. The nobility
4. The commoners
5. Landless peasants
6. Slaves
Slaves were able to raise their social class.
They also had pictographical writing.
They inherited the Pok-A-Tok game from the Mayans.
The end of the game likely involved some form of sacrifice.

## Toltec
Established between the 10 to mid-12 Century.
There capital was at Tollan.
They rose from the Tolteca-Chichimeca people who had migrate from the deserts of the North-West of to Culhuacan in the valley of Mexico.
They were succeeded by the Aztecs.
### Economic history
They practised subsistence farming with the ability to produce huge maize crops.
They also grew naturally coloured cotton of red, yellow, green and blue.2
They involved themselves with heavy trade of obsidian for blades and arrowheads.
They had highly skilled craftsmen skilled in:
1. Metallurgy
2. Pottery

### Social History/ Culture
1. They constructed great pyramids.
2. Palaces were constructed for their leaders

### Decline

## The Inca
They practised terrace farming.
It began at the small kingdom of Cusco located at the mountains of Peru.
The Inca had a very specifically formulated government that was highly efficient.
They never had a writing system but used a knot system to convey information.
Unlike the other Meso-American civilization they had no form of writing.
They learnt how to freeze dry food
They also were able to consistently perform successful brain surgery.
