Happened in the __Indus valley__ 

## Achievements

1. They developed a form of __writing__.
2. __Urbanization__ - they built cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro
3. __Construction skills__- towns were surrounded by high and thick brick walls and streets were laid out in a grid pattern.
4.  Waste management
5. Strong __central management__- the leader properly lived in an elevated structure known as the citadel.
6. They had __advanced storage facilities__ characterized by well ventilation for storing grains.
7. __Trade__- the Indus valley veul stones have been found in Egypt
8. __Architecture__ - foundations of large public buildings were laid using mud bricks baked using a kiln because sun baked bricks would dissolve during flooding.
9. __Iron smelting technology__
10. __Water transport__ - use of rivers to move goods and people
