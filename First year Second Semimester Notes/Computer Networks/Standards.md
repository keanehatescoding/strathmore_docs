The IEEE(Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is the board is responsible for creation of wireless networking standards i.e WiFi which uses IEEE 802.11 standard.
The WiFi Alliance is the alliance responsible for testing wireless LAN devices. If you see the WiFi symbol in a device then it means it was successfully tested and verified by the WiFi Alliance.
IETF (Internet Engineering Task Force) is responsible for publishing and managing RFCs for different protocols. 
[[IANA]] (Internet Assignment Number Authority) is responsible for assigning and removing ipv4 and ipv6 addresses.
ICANN(Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers) is responsible for assigning and managing ports.
