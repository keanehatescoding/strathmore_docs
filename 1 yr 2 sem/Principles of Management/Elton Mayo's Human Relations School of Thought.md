Elton Mayo's was a strong believer of Fredrick Taylor's Scientific Management Approach(Taylorism). However, he disagreed on Taylor's thought that financial incentives were the mainly motivation for employees.
Elton Mayo initially believed that physical factors influenced the morale of employees. He created a series of experiment known as the Hawthorne studies that tested this belief.
His conclusion were he was wrong as physical factors like lighting has little to negative correlation with the productivity of employees.
He concluded the social factors and not physical factors that helped boost the morale of employees.
Positive relationships between managers and employees were found to be key in motivation.
Participants productivity increased when they felt valued and appreciated.
This Hawthorne experiments led to the creation of the very first HR department

## Critics

1. The main consideration in the productivity of these experiments was the output of the employees.
2. Many scientists consider this studies was done in an unscientific manner.
3. The number of participants in the experiment was too little.
4. It ignores the potential impact of conflict in the organization and assumes every employee/manager coexist in a harmony with each other.
5. Original findings lack sufficient supporting evidence.
6. It oversimplifies human behaviour- it assumes satisfied employees will automatically produce the best output.
