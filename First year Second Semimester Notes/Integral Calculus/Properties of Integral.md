1. __Homogeneity of Integration/ Scalar multiplication property of an integral__
(i)
$$\int k f(x)dx = k \int f(x)dx$$
(ii)
$$\int - f(x)dx = - \int f(x)dx$$ -> $k = -1$  from the 1st property

2. __Linearity or Additivity property of integration __
$$\int f(x)dx + \int g(x)dx = \int (f(x)+g(x))dx$$
$$\int f(x)dx - \int g(x)dx = \int (f(x)-g(x))dx$$  it implies $\int g(x)dx = k \int  g(x)$
