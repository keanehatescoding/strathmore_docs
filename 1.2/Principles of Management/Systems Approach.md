This is a management perspective that emphasizes organizations should be looked as entire systems as a whole that is made up of a hierarchy of subsystems.
A defined system has a clear boundary and is separate from the environment.

## Advantages

1. It helps to see the system as a whole and resolve the issues.
2. This approach helps understand the problem and come up with possible solutions.
3. It clearly defines the scope of a systems and problems the system has influence on.

## Disadvantages

1. At times the system is too complex and it is much more convenient to look at the subsystems rather then the entire system.
2. Important details may be omitted as a result of focusing on the entire system as holistic.
3. Many times when trying to solve an issue the focus is usually on one component and not the entire system.
