
## Sources

1. Legitimate/ Positional power- this is the influence a person(s) has by virtue of the position is an organization.
2. Reward power- this is the power to reward or withhold various kinds of rewards.
3. Coercive power - this is the power to enforce work/compliance by using emotional, psychological or physical threats.
4. Expert power - this is influence over a group of people due to high technical expertise in a specific domain of knowledge.
5. Referent power - this is influence due to personal traits that inspire admiration, respect and loyalty from others.

A __leader__ is someone who influences his/her followers towards the attainment of their desired goals while a __manager__ is someone who is responsible for managing the entire organization.
A leader inspires/motivates while a manager directs/controls people.

## Leaders vs Managers

1. Leaders are foresighted visionaries while managers are quick in decision making.
2. Leaders set direction and their followers while managers plan activities.
3. Managers are transactional i.e work for pay while leaders are transformational.
4. Managers try to make employees as efficient as possible while leaders try to make their followers as effective as possible.
5. Managers are focus on the process while leaders focus on people.
6. Managers react to change while leaders promote change.
7. Managers try to minimize conflict while leaders use conflict as an asset, i.e what lesson can they learn from conflict.
8. Managers' perspectives are short ranged while leaders perspectives are long ranged.
