## 1. Homogeneity of Integration/ Scalar multiplication property of an integral
(i)
$$\int k f(x)dx = k \int f(x)dx$$
(ii)
$$\int - f(x)dx = - \int f(x)dx$$ For (ii) it implies $k = -1$.

## 2. Linearity or Additivity property of integration
(i)$$\int f(x)dx + \int g(x)dx = \int f(x)+g(x)dx$$
(ii)$$\int f(x)dx - \int g(x)dx = \int f(x)-g(x)dx$$in (ii) it implies $-\int g(x)dx = k \int  g(x)$ where $k = -1$

$$\int \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} \ne \frac{\int f(x)}{ \int g(x)} $$
