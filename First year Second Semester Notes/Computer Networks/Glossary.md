__Time of service__ used to determine the priority of the packet. I.e telephony packets are usually are given higher priority over normal packets.
__Fragment flag__ use to show if that is the last fragment of the data being sent i.e if it is not equal to zero then it means it is not the last packet.
__SSID__ - Service Set Identifier which is the name of the wireless network.
__Socket__ - the combination of a source ip and source port or the combination of the destination ip and destination port i.e 192.168.0.2:1099 or 8.8.4.4:53.
__Socket Pair__ - the combination of a source socket and a destination socket.
__Packet switching__ - data is broken down into small units called packets and transmitted via different routes.
__Circuit switching__ - a dedicated route is established before communication and only this route is used.
__Converged network__ - a network transporting different types of data i.e video, voice, data. i.e. for cost savings
__Encapsulation__ - is the adding of a header to a PDU at each layer of TCP/IP or OSI model before transmitting it over a media.
__Decapsulation__ - is the removal and processing of headers from a PDU at each layer of the OSI or TCP/IP model once we receive it over on another host.
<abbr title="Protocol Data Unit"><b>PDU</b></abbr> -  data unit is the name of the blob of data plus encapsulated header(s) at the specified layer of the TCP/IP or OSI model.
