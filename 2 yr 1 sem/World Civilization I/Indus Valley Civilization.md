Happened in the __Indus river valley__ which was a fertile flood plain.
It flourished from approximately 2900 BC to 1800 BC.

## Achievements

1. They developed a form of __writing__ which involved more than 400 signs.
2. __Urbanization__ - they built cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro with a grid pattern layout, drainage system, brick houses.
3. __Construction skills__- towns were surrounded by high and thick brick walls and streets were laid out in a grid pattern.
4. __Waste management__ - they had covered drainage systems hence some sanitization.
5. Strong __central management__- the leader properly lived in an elevated structure known as the __citadel__ which had a different structure from normal houses.
6. They had __advanced storage facilities__ characterized by well ventilation for storing grains.
7. __Trade__- the Indus valley seal stones have been found in Tigris-Euphrates valley. Indus valley merchants are speculated to be middle men between long distance trade from west-central Asia and the Persian Gulf.
8. __Irrigation__ - they had impressive irrigation skills where they made use of canals and the potter's wheel.
9. __Architecture__ - foundations of large public buildings were laid using mud bricks baked using a kiln because sun baked bricks would dissolve during flooding.
10. __Iron smelting technology__
11. __Water transport__ - use of rivers to move goods and people

## Collapse of the Indus Civilization

Some of the reasons for the collapse of the Indus Valley Civilisation were:
1. Breakdown of fragile inter-relationships of the political, social and economic systems that certain order and prosperity.
2. __Natural Disaster__ i.e possibly an earthquake or massive flooding
3. Urban centers could not be sustained therefore village farming and herding replace this civilization.
