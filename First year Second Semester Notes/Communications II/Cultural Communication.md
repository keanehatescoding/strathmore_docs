__Intercultural communication__ is Communication that takes place between different cultural backgrounds but living in the same country.
__Cross cultural communication__ happens when parties engage in negotiation or any other form of communication but these parties are from different countries or backgrounds.
__Multicultural communication__ -occurs when people from different cultural, ethnic, racial and religious backgrounds communicate with one another by making special adjustments to different cultural standards through tolerance and sensitivity.
In Japanese culture the greater the bow the higher the level of respect.
__Cross cultural pragmatics__ what is implied vs what is actually said. i.e bite me means a like metaphorically fight me in American culture but in other cultures it could mean fight me.
__Ethnocentrism__ the belief that your culture is better than other's/
## How to overcome cultural barriers
1. Know your culture first- you can love someone else unless you love yourself first. This is because foreigners may ask you about your culture. 
2. Practise active listening
3. Look beyond stereotypes.
4. Keep an unbiased mind.
5. Don't be ethnocentric and judgemental.
6. Pay attention to non verbal cues
7. Take interest in other people's culture
8. Pay attention to how words are used.
