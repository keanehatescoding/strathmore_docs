Generally speaking, asymmetric encryption algorithms rely on modulo arithmetic.
A operation $o$ is commutative IFF $xoy = yox\in x,y$ 
Commutative operations:
1. Integer addition
2. Integer multiplication
3. Paint Mixing

Generally, non-commutative operations:
1. Integer subtraction
2. Integer division
3. Matrix multiplication

## Examples
1. Diffie Hellman Key Exchange
2. RSA
3. Digital Signature Standard- uses the Digital Signature Algorithm(DSA)
4. Ellliptic-curve Cryptography
5. ElGamal encryption system
