There are 3 types of buses:
1. The control bus
2. The address bus
3. The data bus
The control bus is used to synchronize all the actions in all of the devices attached to it.
The address bus helps to address of instructions in data that are being transferred between the cpu and memory.
The data bus is used to handle the transfer of instructions data between the cpu,memory and etc.
They may also be an i/o bus.
