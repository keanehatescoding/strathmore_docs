Thomas Aquinas believed in the existence of God.
He also knew not everyone had been exposed to the bible therefore he theorized there must be a way for people to know the laws of God without a bible. 
__Natural law__ is a theory by Thomas Aquinas that states __God made us pre-loaded with the need to know what's good__.
We all want stuff and according to Aquinas that is okay.
Since God is Awesome therefore He instilled within us a intuitive desire for the things that He saw would be the best for them.
The things that humans desire are known as the basic needs and are:
1. Life / self preservation
2. Reproduction
3. Educate one's offspring
4. Seek God
5. Live in society
6. Avoid offense
7. Shun ignorance
According to this theory we don't need church, the bible or class in order to understand the natural law, instead our instincts show us the basic goods, and reason allows us to derive the natural law from them.
Every basic good has a prohibition i.e life is the basic good therefore it's prohibition is do not kill.
Likewise every prohibition has a positive injunction i.e do not kill has the positive injunction promote life.
From a single positive injunction we can infer different meanings i.e from promote life we can infer help the less fortunate, feed the hungry etc.
Natural law is therefore derived from this positive injunctions.
Thomas Aquinas justifies his natural law theory by saying people at times refuse to follow the natural law because of emotions and ignorance.
Sometimes people think something is right therefore follow it even though this is not true i.e doctors advised blood letting as a form of medication.
Aquinas also argued although we are rational creatures we are also emotional creatures.
