## Brief Explanation
This is a company structure where each multiple teams report to different leaders.

Examples
An IT company that has various departments like research, development, security and finance branch.
A drinks company that has various teams and department like testing, research, manufacture, finance and sales.

Advantages
1. It creates open communication between team members which allows innovative solutions
2. It creates free flow of information since each team reports to different team leaders which in turn could lead to increased productivity and collaboration
3. It allows the efficient use of resources by inclusion of specialists from other teams to a separate team which in turn leads overhead costs.
Disadvantages
1. It may lead to a complex reporting style since team members may have trouble knowing who to and what to report to.
2. It could lead to slow response time which leads to further delays due to requirement of reporting to multiple people.
3. The task of juggling priorities between the project managers and department heads could be hard especially when both consider them important.
