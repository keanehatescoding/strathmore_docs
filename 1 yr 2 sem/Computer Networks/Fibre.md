There are two main types of fibre optics cables namely:
1. Single mode Fiber(SMF)
2. Multimode Fiber(MMF)
## <abbr title="Single Mode Fiber">SMF</abbr>
It uses a laser technology to send a single ray of light.
It also uses a yellow jacket.
It is more expensive than <abbr title="multimode fiber">MMF</abbr>.
It is popular for long distance data transmission.
![[{109B67FF-1EFF-4FC4-B61F-0651A13EFD34}.png]]
## <abbr title="multimode fiber">MMF</abbr>
It uses an <abbr title="light emiting diode">LED</abbr> as opposed to a single laser in <abbr title="single mode fiber">SMF</abbr>.
It uses a orange(or aqua) jacket.
It is cheaper than <abbr title="Single Mode Fiber">SMF</abbr>.

## Optical-Fiber Connectors
An optical-fiber connector terminates the end of a optical fiber.
They are classified according to dimensions and methods coupling.

## Advantages
1. Highest bandwidth
2. Longest range
3. Immune to <abbr title="electromagnetic interference">EMI</abbr> and <abbr title="radio Frequency Interference">RFI</abbr>

## Disadvantages
1. It is more expensive than copper.
2. It requires a higher skill to install.
3. It is more fragile than coper.
