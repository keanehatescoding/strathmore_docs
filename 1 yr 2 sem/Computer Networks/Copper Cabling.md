There are 3 types of copper cables used in networking.
1. Unshielded Twisted Pair(UTP)
2. Shielded Twisted Pair(STP)
3. Coaxial Cable
The electrical characteristics of copper cabling are defined by <abbr title="Institue of Electirical and Electronics Engineers">IEEE</abbr> and it places them into different categories according to bandwidth. 
## <abbr title="Unshielded Twisted Pair">UTP</abbr>
It is usually crimped(terminated) using a male component known as a <b>RJ45</b> connector via a crimping tool.
The TIA/EIA-568 standard describes the wire color codes to pin assignments (pinouts) for Ethernet cables.
