## Kirchhoff's Current Law

The algebraic sum of currents meeting at a point is zero.
$$\sum{i}=0$$

## Kirchhoff's Voltage Law

In any closed electrical circuit the algebraic sum of product of current and resistance and the sum of EMF connected in it equal to zero.
$$\sum{rise}-\sum{drop}=0$$
When current is flowing from a positive terminal to a negative terminal EMF is positive(- to + means increasing) and vice versa.
