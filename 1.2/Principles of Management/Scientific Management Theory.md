
This theory is also known as __Taylorism__.

## Features

1. Main motivation is financial compensation
2. Science could be used to increase productivity and efficiency
3. Workers should be given one task and they should master that task
4. Their is only one best way of doing a task.
5. Time and motion studies where analysing a task and finding the shortest way to complete it.
6. Employees need not to be skilled at a task but they just needed to be highly productive at a task.
7. Financial compensation should be based on productivity.
8. Those that could not be at par with the most productive should be fired.
9. Standardisation.

## Advantages

1. Increased productivity.
2. Maximised profit.
## Disadvantages

1. Has lead to a lot of monotony.
2. Leads to unemployment.
3. Leads to lower creativity.
4. Dehumanizes humans.
