In ASK the amplitude of the carrier varies with respect to the amplitude of the message signal.
To get the ASK signal we multiply the carrier by the message signal. Since the message signal is binary stream of ones and zeros, when m = 0 no signal is transmitted as $0\times \cos(\omega_{c)}t=0$ while when m = 1 then the signal being transmitted is $\cos{\omega_{c}}$ since $1\times \cos(\omega_{c)}= \cos(\omega_c)$ 
In ASK demodulation there are 2 techniques used namely:
1. Synchronous
2. Asynchronous
