## 1. Pre-reading phase
Setting the stage for a meaningful reading experience
Why are you reading this text?
1. To look for specific information
2. Understand a new concept
3. For pleasure
be intentional not just passive.
Scan the text before you start reading.
Scanning involves looking for any titles, heading, subheading -> visuals.
Scanning helps you create a mental map of what your reading.

## 2. Active Reading phase
1. Circle key words
2. Underline important claims
3. Use symbols to mark agreement and disagreement
4. If you can't write directly on the text use sticky notes or separate notebook
### Highlighting
Don't highlight everything. Be strategic, ask yourself is this something that you are going to need to refer to or does it highlight a key concept.

## 3. Post Reading Reflection Phase
Ask yourself what did you get from this text.
Don't be afraid of going back to your notes they can be invaluable.
See how what you got from this text fits with your existing knowledge. Did you strengthen your opinions or did the text change your opinion.
