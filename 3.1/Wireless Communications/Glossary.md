**Link Budget** - the mathematical balance between how much power is sent versus how much power is lost.
**Ionospheric 'reflection'** - em waves are not reflected but they bend (refract) in the ionosphere and return to earth.
**Multi-hop transmission** - em waves successively bounce between the ionosphere and between the Earth and in turn allow very long-distance communication.
**Multi-path interference** - a type of interference that occurs when signals arriving via different paths at different times.
**Uplink** - transmission from the earth station to the satellite.
**Downlink** - tranmission from the satelitte to the earth station
**Eccentricity** - it defines how stretched out an ellipse is from a perfect circle as is given by $e=\frac{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{a}$
**Equivalent Isotropic Radiated Power(EIRP)** is the power that a theoretical isotropic antenna would emit to produce peak power density observed in the direction of maximum antenna gain.
