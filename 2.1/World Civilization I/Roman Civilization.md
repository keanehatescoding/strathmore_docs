## Ancient Roman Civilization
They stressed on morality, stressing the importance  of nature & living within one's means.
All of them were farmers No matter what you did you had to farm. Divorce was unheard of until the late republican era. Roles were strictly defined within the Roman family
The Roman matron were more free compared to the Greece i.e woman were allowed to attend and preside ceremonies and monitored the education of both their male and female offspring.
Every family had an undying fire burning on it's hearth to symbolic to the goddess Vesta to ensure the continuity of the family.
### Etruscans and the Greeks
The Etruscans conquered the Romans and in the process the Romans learnt a lot from the Etruscans. They even developed their Roman alphabet from the Etruscans.
