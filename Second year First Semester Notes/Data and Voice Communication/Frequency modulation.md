This is a type of modulation whereby only the frequency of the carrier signal is varied to represent the frequency of the message being transmitted i.e
If the function of the modulated signal is given by
$$s(t)=A_{c}\cos(\omega(t)t+\phi_{c}t)$$
then $\omega(t)= F(m(t))$
$$\omega (t)=2\pi f_{c}+2\pi k_{f}\int_{0}^{t}{x(t)dt}$$
$k_f$ is known as the __frequency sensitivity__ and it's units are
__Hertz per Volts__.
The modulation index $\beta$ is given by:
$$\beta = \frac {\triangle f}{f_m}$$
Where $\triangle f$ is the while $f_m$ is the frequency of the modulating signal.
