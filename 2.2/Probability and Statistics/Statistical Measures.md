## Measures of Dispersion
__Dispersion__ is the spread out of data points.
It deals with the spread out of data points from the central measure tendency:
It is classified into 2:
1. Absolute measures
2. Relative measures

### 1. Absolute measure of dispersion
1. The range
2. Interquartile range & quartile deviation
3. Mean deviation or average deviation
4. Standard deviation

### 2. Relative measures of dispersion
1. Coefficient of range
2. Coefficient of quartile deviation
3. Coefficient of mean deviation
4. Coefficient of variation

#### Range
Range = Largest$(L)$ - Smallest$(S)$
Co-efficient of Range
$$CR = \frac{L-S}{L+S}$$
Range for __grouped data__ is the difference of the upper limit in the last class and the lower limit in the first class.
##### Limitations
1. It is based only on two values and does not cover all the items in a distribution
2. It is influenced by an extreme value(non robust)
3. It fails to give an idea about the pattern of distribution
