The Cartesian Product of a set is a set of ordered pairs such that the first element of the pair is the first element of the product and the second element of the pair is the second element of the product.
$$A\times B = \{(x,y), x\in A, y\in B\}$$
Any subset of a Cartesian product is a relation.

## Cartesian Product of n sets
$$D_{1\times}D_2\times...\times D_n=\{(d_1,d_2,...,d_n),d_{1}\in D_{1},d_{1} \in D_{2}, ...,d_{n}\in D_n$$
