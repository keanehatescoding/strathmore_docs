## Periodicity
$$\sin(x+2\pi)= \sin(x)$$
$$\cos(x+2\pi)=\cos(x)$$
## Odd functions
$$f(-x)=-f(x)\in x$$
i.e sin
$\sin(-x)=-\sin(x)$
## Even functions
$$f(x) = f(-x)$$
i.e cosine
$\cos(x)=\cos(-x)$
## Series expansion
$$\sin(-x)= x- \frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}-...$$
$$\cos=x-\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}-...$$
