Macedonians spoke a separate language from Greeks and Macedonians never considered themselves as Greeks while the Greeks considered them as barbarians.
Macedonian nobles learned Greek and identified themselves as Greeks.
Two Macedonian Kings, Philip II and his son Alexander the Great filled the power vacuum in Greece by turning their formally weak kingdom to an international super kingdom.
Alexander the Great become the king of Macedonia after his father Philip II was assassinated in 336 BC.
He took immediate measures to quell insurrections both domestic and abroad.
Alexander the Great considered himself as a superhuman and demanded he was worshiped as a god.
He died from a sickness and he never had a son so his generals took over after his death.

## The transformation of Greece
### Hellenistic Greece (323 - 331 BC)
The ancient name of Greece is __Hellen__.
Characterized when the successor kings who were generals took over the kingdom after Alexander the Great's death.
They usually let city-states keep their internal freedom so long as they followed kings' foreign policies.
They were dictatorial.
#### Antigonous
Founded a kingdom encompassing parts of Asia Minor, the Middle East, Macedonia and Greece.

#### Seleucus I
Established rule over Babylon.

#### Ptolomy I
Established rule over Egypt
Egypt was the last area to be conquered by the Romans in 31 BC when under Queen Cleopatra.

## Roman Greece
Greece became reconciled to Roman Rule.
The Emperors allowed Greeks to join their Senate.
The Roman Empire split into two because it become impossible to defend the kingdom from the Persian and German invaders.
