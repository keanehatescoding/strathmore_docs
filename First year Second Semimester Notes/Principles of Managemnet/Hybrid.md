“Hybrid” means combining two different things. Hybrid structure and and matrix structure and more or less the same.

Examples 
Starbucks is an example of a business with a hybrid organizational structure, combining functional, geographic, and product-based structures.
Google thrives on a unique blend of hierarchical and flat structures.

Advantages
1.  These organizations blend structured hierarchy with operational freedom. This balance optimizes efficiency and employee productivity.
2. Hybrid organizational structures assign personnel and assets where needed most. When a project requires specific skills, hybrid structures can form specialized teams. Once the project concludes, these teams disband, and resources reallocate to new initiatives.

Disadvantages
1. Different parts of the organization often have conflicting priorities. These conflicts can complicate the alignment of corporate objectives. 
2. In a hybrid structure, resource conflicts arise when employees work on many projects, causing tension between departments.
