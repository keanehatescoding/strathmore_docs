These are the channels ways data is sent from one node to another. There are 3 primary types of network media.
1. Wireless transmission i.e WiFi, Bluetooth
2. Metal in wires i.e twisted-pair, coaxial
3. Glass or plastic i.e in Fiber-optics
## Criteria when choosing networking media
1. Environmental/Weather conditions
2. Speed
3. Bandwidth
4. Cost of the medium and installation
