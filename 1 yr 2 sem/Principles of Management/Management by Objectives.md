Is a strategic management model that aims to improve the performance of an organization by clearly defining the objectives that agreed both by the managers and employees.

## Steps

1. Determine or revise the objectives of the entire organisation.
2. Translate the organisational objectives to the employee.
3. Employees should be encouraged to help set their own objectives to reach these larger organizational goals.
4. Monitor the progress of the employees.
5. Evaluate and reward employee progress.
## Advantages

1. Employees take pride in their own work and are assigned work which they know they can achieve.
2. Assigning tailored goals gives a sense of importance to the employee in turn boosting performance and loyalty.
3. Relations between employees and managers increases.

## Disadvantages

1. It's emphasis on objectives often leaves other parts of the company such as company culture.
2. Strain is increased on employees to reach their goals.
4. Often leads to shortcuts in order to achieve company targets which in turn reduces product quality.
5. If a company relies solely on MBO for all management responsibilities, it can be problematic in roles that cannot fit under MBO.
