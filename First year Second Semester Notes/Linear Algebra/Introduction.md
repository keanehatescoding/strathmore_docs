A system of linear equations is a...
Any solvable system of linear equation can be written as $A\vec{x}=\vec{v}$ where matrix $A$ is the coefficient matrix,
$$\matrix A = \begin{bmatrix} i_{1} & i_{2} & ...  &  i_{n-1} & i_{n}\\ j_{1} & j_{2} & ...  &  j_{n-1} & j_{n}\\ k_{i} & k_{2} & ...  & k_{n-1} & k_{n} \\ .._{1}& .._{2} & ... & _{n-1} & .._{n} \end{bmatrix}$$

vector $\vec x$ is a vector containing all the variables in the system of equations,
$$\vec x = \begin{pmatrix} x  \\ y \\ z  \\ ..\end{pmatrix}$$

and a constant vector $\vec v$ which result of the product between matrix $\matrix A$ and vector $\vec x$.
$$\vec v=\begin{pmatrix}c_{1} \\ c_{2} \\ c_3 \\ ..\end{pmatrix}$$
$A\vec{x}=\vec{v}$
An __invertible matrix__ is a square matrix that has a inverse or a square matrix with a determinant not equal to zero.
