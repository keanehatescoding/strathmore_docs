Digital signals can be transmitted via 2 ways namely:
1. __Baseband transmission__
2. __Broadbadn transmission__

## Baseband Tranmission

Transmits digital signals without changing it to analog signals.
It requires a channel that has a bandwidth that start with zero.
It requires a dedicated medium i.e only one signal can be sent at a time not multiple channels.
The bandwidth is directly proportional to the bit rate.

### Examples:

1.Wired <abbr title="Local Area Networks">LANs</abbr>

## Broadband Transmission

To transmit the digital signal it requires you to first change it to an analog signal.
It uses a channel that does not start with zero.
It does not use a dedicated channel and multiple channels are sent within the medium.

### Example

1. Telephone lines used to send cellular data
