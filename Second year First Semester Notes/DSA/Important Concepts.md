__Time complexity__ - the rate of increase in the number of steps your algorithm takes when an additional element/node is added to original data structure.
__Space complexity__ - the rate of increase in space that your algorithm uses when it additional element/node is added to your data structure.

__NB: O(1) != always 1 step__ it just means a constant time access no  number the number of additional elements/nodes added to the original data structure.z

__Big $O$ notation__ is a mathematical notation that describes the __limiting behaviour__ of a function as the number of arguments of the function as the number of arguments tends towards a particular value or infinity. The letter __O__ is used because the growth rate of a function is known as the __order of the function__.
