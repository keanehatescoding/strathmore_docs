It is a stream cipher with byte-oriented operations.
It is based on the use of a random permutation of **8-bit** numbers from 0 to 255.
Expected period is greater than $10^{100}$
Created by Ron Rivest.
First few bytes are non-random, depending on how keys are generated.

## Applications
1. SSL and WEP(IEEE 802.11)
