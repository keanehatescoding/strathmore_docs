Virtual Local Area Network or VLAN is technology that enables multiple networks to be created in a single switch i.e 192.168.0.* , 10.0.0.* and 134.44.3.* on the same switch.
Each VLAN is not aware of the other VLAN and that is why it needs a router to connect two or more VLAN.
The connection that connects the VLAN and the Router is called the trunk.
## Benefits
1. More cost effective as opposed to buying multiple networking equipment for each network.
2. Security since each VLAN is isolated and not aware of any other VLAN on the switch.
