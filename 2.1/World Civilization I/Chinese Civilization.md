## Economic Activity
1. Subsistence farming- growing millet, wheat rice, kept poultry and raised pigs.
2. Pot making - pottery on a wheel and fired it at high temperatures
3. Silk cloth production
4. Bronze metallurgy
5. Mining - copper and tin were mined for bronze metallurgy.

## Shang Dynasty
It had a warrior class, where the king ruled the core area of the empire directly.
It was characterized by frequent military campaigns that provided the warrior aristocracy with a theatre of brave achievements and yielded considerable plunder.

### Achievements
1. It established trade routes with china and beyond.
2. It brought varied commodities i.e ivory and jewelry from wars.
3. There was an indication Mesopotamia and the Shang Dynasty were in contact hence sharing of ideas.
4. They had a religious background which involved both animal sacrifice and human sacrifice.
5. Class system - bronze objects were reserved for members of the elite while rural peasants used stone tools. Also their complex pictorial writing system was only available to the elite.
6. They evolved horse drawn chariots, which they may have adapted from Mesopotamia and used them in warfare.
7. The dynasty is also credited with artisans who skillfully made and beautifully decorated objects.
8. They evolved great architectural skills which they used to build great defensive walls, monumental royal tombs and to construct cities.
9. They developed a pictorial form of writing which involved hundreds of signs hence needed a lot of time to master therefore it was the hallmark of the educated elite class.
## The Zhou Period
The Shang Dynasty lasted 6 centuries and was finally conquered by Duke Wilof Zhou.
The Zhou preserved the foundations of culture created by the other predecessors but incorporated new elements.
They created a new chief duty that referred to as Heaven and the king become son of Heaven and his rule was mandate of Heaven.
He would retain the backing of the gods as long as he served as a wise, principled and energetic guardian of his people.
They separated religion form politics which allowed China to develop important secular philosophies.
They had rule system that was __decentralized__.
During 600 BC iron began replacing bronze as the primary metal for tools and weapons.
They had a code of laws written down.
Concept of private property emerged, land belonged to the father and was divided equally among the sons when he passed on.

### Confucius' Teaching
Ceremony is important.
We should treat our parents with reverence - __filial piety__.
We should be obedient to honorable people - the relationship between rulers and subjects must be like the grass and wind, it bends when it blows. Let masters be master, subjects be subjects, fathers be fathers and sons be sons.
Cultivated knowledge can be more important than creativity.
Don't do unto others what you don't want done to yourself
