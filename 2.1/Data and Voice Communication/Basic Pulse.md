There are 3 basic pulses:
1. <abbr title="Non return to Zero"><b>NRZ</b></abbr> - within the time $T_b$ the pulse does not return to zero.
2. <abbr title="Return to Zero"><b>RZ</b></abbr> - pulse occupies half of the bit duration $\frac {T_b}{2}$
3. __Manchester Pulse__ - there will be a transition of amplitude from $+A$ to $-A$ or vice versa at half duration i.e. $\frac{T_b}{2}$
