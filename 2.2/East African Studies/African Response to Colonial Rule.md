## Primary Resitors
The Nandi 
## Secondary Resitors
They initialy welcomed the Europeans and began resisteing when they realised the Europeans took their independence
They include the Kabaka Mwanga of the Baganda.

## Collaborators
They were communities who cooperated with the Europeans.
## Oportunists
They include Apollo Kagwa and Semei Kakungulu of Baganda.
## Don't carers
Some communities also did not mind whether they were colonised or not.
## Reasons for Collaboration
1. They thought they would safeguard their independence.
2. They never expected the Europeans would betray them.
3. They never understood the signing of treaties and protection would take away their independence.
4. The Maasai were weakened by smallpox and Rinderpest hence they collaborated in order to get advantages from their weakened position.
5. Some African communities collaborated because of missionary influence.
