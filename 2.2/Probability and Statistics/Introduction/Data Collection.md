## Sampling
__Sampling__ is a process used in statistical analysis for selecting a sample from the population.
### Sampling Techniques
1. __Probability Sampling__: Every item in a population of interest, has a known, and usually equal chance of being chosen for inclusion in the sample. Example:
 i. __Simple random sampling__ - all items in the population have equal chance of being selected.
ii. __Stratified random sampling__ - Divide the population into groups called strata and then take a sample from each stratum).
iii. __Cluster random sampling__ - Divide the population into clusters and then randomly select some of the clusters. All the members from these clusters are in the cluster sample.
iv. __Systematic random sampling__ - randomly select a starting point and take every $n^{\text {th}}$ piece of data from a listing of the population.
2. __Non-probability Sampling__: Sample does not have known probability of being selected.
Example:
i. __Convenience sampling__ - A convenience sample is made up of measurements, or observations that are most easily accessible.
ii. __Judgmental sampling__ - one in which an individual selects the items to be included depending on his judgment in the sample
