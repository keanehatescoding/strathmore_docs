The minimum ethernet frame size is __64__ bytes and the expected maximum is __1518__ bytes. The preamble is not included when describing the size of the frame.
A frame less than 64 bytes in size is known as a __runt frame__ or __collision fragment__ , while a frame larger then 1500 bytes is considered a __"jumbo"__ or __"baby giant frames"__ and is dropped by default.
Jumbos are supported by Fast Ethernet and Gigabit Ethernet switches and <abbr title='network interface card'>NICs</abbr>.
