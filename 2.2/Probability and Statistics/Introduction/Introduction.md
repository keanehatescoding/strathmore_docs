Statistics is the science of conducting studies to collect, organize, analyze, summarize & draw conclusions from data.
## Reason for Learning Statistics
1. To summarize business data
2. To draw conclusions from data
3. To make reliable forecast about business activities.
4. To improve business process(quality control)
## Types of statistics
1. __Descriptive statistics__ - deals with the collection, organizations, summarization & presentation of data i.e tabular, graphical and summary statistics.
2. __Inferential statistics__ - deals with analyzing sample data to draw conclusions about a population e.g. estimation and hypothesis testing statistics.
## Measures of scale
Measurement scales are used to categorize and/or
quantify variables.
### Definition (Properties of Measurement Scales)
__Identity__: Each value on the measurement scale has a unique meaning.
__Magnitude__: Values on the measurement scale have an ordered relationship to one another. That is, some values are larger and some are smaller.
__Equal intervals__: Scale units along the scale are equal to one another. This means, for example, that the difference between 1 and 2 would be equal to the difference between 19 and 20.
__Absolute zero__: The scale has a true zero point, below which no values exist.

# Based on Nature
## Types of data
1. Qualitative data(Categorical)
2. Quantitative data(Numerical)

### Qualitative data (categorical)
In qualitative data, variables can be placed in distinct categories according to characteristics.
Is further classified into 2:
1. __Nominal data__ - data that has no natural ordering of categories i.e beauty, races, people. Nominal data only has __identity property__ i.e each value is unique i.e black, white, Chinese for race.
2. __Ordinal data__ - data that has a natural ordering of categories i.e product quality rating, a grading system. This type of data has both an identity and a magnitude i.e a A grade is better than B and both are unique. 


### Quantitative data
1. __Discrete data__ - values that can be counted, i.e number of cars, toys etc.
2. __Continuous data__ - values that can be measured but not counted i.e height, distance etc.

## Types of Measures of Scale
### 1. Nominal Scale of Measurement
Only satisfied the identity property.
Classifies the data into categories which no order or ranking can be imposed on the data e.g. gender, race etc.

### 2. Ordinal scale of Measurement
It satisfies the property of identity and magnitude.
Data can be ordered but cannot be measured e.g. a grading system or a product rating system.


### 3. Interval scale of Measurement
It has the properties of identity, magnitude, and equal intervals.
Data with a definite ordering but no starting point; the differences can be measured e.g. __temperature__.

### 4. Ratio Scale of Measurement
Satisfies all four of the properties of measurement: identity, magnitude, equal intervals, and an absolute zero.
Data with a starting point that can be ordered; the differences have meaning and ratios can be calculated e.g. height, age, weight.
