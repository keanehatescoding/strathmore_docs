## Types of Computer Ethics

1. __Proffesional ethics__ ensuring work done by engineers is of the highest quality and meets ethical standards.
2. __Social ethics__ involves considering the impact of tech on the society as a whole and not hurt any individual or group.
3. __Personal Ethics__ 
You should also consider the ethical considerations with regards to privacy, security and intellectual property.
Integrity - is the adherence of a set of rules/principles.

## A. Deontology

It determines right and wrong based on a set of clearly defined rules.
A core principle of deontology is everyone has a inherent worth and dignity and should be treated with fairness and dignity
Another core principle is __universability__ which states that moral principles are universally applicable and impartial.
Shortcome: It is too rigid especially when a certain crime may have been necessary.

## B.Consequentialism

It states that the morality of an action is concluded by it's consequences.
Advantages: It is more adaptive and uses context to consider the repurcussions.
Shortcome: It's could possibly lead to unethical behaviour if the end of a result it emphasized over the means to reach the ends.

## C. Teleological

It emphasizes the importance of considering the consequences of an actions. 
It considers the impact of technology on the environment and the society and strives on creating effective and sustainable solutions.


## Theories

### A. Unitarian

It suggests the moral value of an action is measured by it's capability to maximize overall happiness and minimize overall suffering. 
Unitarianism holds the purpose of engineers is to create technology that benefit the greatest number of people.
