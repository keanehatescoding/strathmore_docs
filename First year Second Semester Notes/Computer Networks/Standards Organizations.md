The <abbr title="Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers"><b>IEEE</b></abbr> is the board is responsible for creation of wireless networking standards i.e WiFi which uses IEEE 802.11 standard and electrical networking standards i.e Ethernet IEEE 802.2.
<abbr title="Internet Engineering Task Force"><b>IETF</b></abbr> is responsible for publishing and managing RFCs for different protocols. 
[[IANA]] (Internet Assignment Number Authority) is responsible for assigning and removing IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.
<abbr title="Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers"><b>ICANN</b></abbr> is responsible for assigning and managing ports.
The WiFi Alliance is the alliance responsible for testing wireless LAN devices. If you see the WiFi symbol in a device then it means it was successfully tested and verified by the WiFi Alliance.
