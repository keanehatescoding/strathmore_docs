The Shannon Capacity Channel Theorem states the maximum rate at which information can be transmitted over a communication channel of a specified bandwidth in the presence of noise.
$$C=2B$$
where $C$ is the channel capacity in bits per second and $B$ is the bandwidth of the channel.
$$$C=2B\log_{2}M$$
$$C=B\log_{2}(1 + \frac {S}{N})$$
where $S$ is the __average signal power__ and $N$ is the __average noise power__.
Alternatively
$$C=3.32\log(1+\frac{S}{N})$$

## Applications
1. In wireless communication it is used to find the maximum amount of data that can be transmitted in a wireless channel without error or data loss.
2. In telecommunications it helps maintain the capacity of telecommunication channel which can help in error communication and build better models.
3. Calculate the amount of information that can be recovered from a signal with a defined noise.
4. In data compression it is used to defined the upper bound for the compression of data or data communication signals.
