## Integral of trig functions
$$\int \cos{ax} \space dx = \frac {1}{a}\sin{ax}+c$$
$$\int \sin{ax}\space dx = \frac {1}{a}-\cos{ax}+c$$
$$\int \tan(x)dx=\ln|\cos(x)|+c$$
$$\int \cot(x)dx=\ln|sin(x)|+c$$
$$\int \sec^{2}ax \space dx= \frac {1}{2}\tan{x} +c$$

$$\int \csc^{2}ax \space dx= \frac {1}{a}-\cot{ax} + c$$
$$\int \sec {ax}\tan{ax} \space dx = \frac {1}{a}\sec {ax}+c$$
$$\int\csc{ax}\cot{ax} \space dx = \frac {1}{2}\csc{ax} + c$$

## Integral of inverse trig functions
$$\int \frac {1}{x^{2}+1}dx= tan^{-1}(x)+c$$
$$\int \frac {1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}dx= \sin^{-1}(x)+c$$
$$\int -\frac {1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}dx= \cos^{-1}(x)+c$$
