Routers and PCs transmit data via pin 1 and 2 while they receive data via pin 3 and 6. This means if you use a normal straight through cable to connect 2 PCs/Routers/Router and PC they won't be able to communicate. Therefore you have to connect them using a cross over cable in order for them to be able to communicate.
Switches on the other hand send data via pin 3 and 6 while they receive data via pin 1 and 2. This means if you connect a straight through cable between a switch and a Pc/router they will are able to communicate while if it were 2 switches then they cannot communicate to each other unless you connect the two switches using a cross over cable.
Modern Routers and switches have a option called Auto-MDIX- these allows them to send and receive data across two similar devices i.e switch & switch or router & router without needing a cross over cable.
